For one weekend in Brussels, young adults try hobbies they have never done before, from pottery to bouldering, in workshops where everyone is a beginner.

Phones stay outside the gate, so everyone goes offline to reconnect with what makes us human: creativity and each other.

You leave with a new hobby and the people you got it with.
